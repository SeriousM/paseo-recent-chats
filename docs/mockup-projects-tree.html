The rule

You pinThe Pinned section shows
a projectthe project, all its workspaces, all their chats
a workspaceits project, the workspace, all its chats
a chatits project, its workspace, that chat
a mixthe union: parents always, children of whatever is pinned. Two of four chats pinned → project, workspace and those two chats.
  • Pinned items stay in the tree as before and also appear in the Pinned section.
  • Parents shown only because of a pinned child are context, not pins: their 📌 stays off. Pinning the parent too then brings in its other children.
  • The Pinned section lists everything it covers (no “+N older”). In the tree, pinned chats never fold into “+N older”, and a pin keeps an old workspace visible under Active only.
  • Quiet projects can be pinned too: click “N quiet projects” to list them.
  • Triage mode's Pinned section lists every chat a pin covers, at any level.

Example pins

  • 2 of 11 chats of the IDP worktree → Everything, the worktree, those 2 chats.
  • The workspace of this plugin → RecentChatsPaseoPlugin, the workspace, its chat.
  • The project ajour-manager on paseo-pai → all 4 workspaces and their chats, including the two Active only hides in the tree.

Storage

Pins become one list of keys (host, project, workspace or chat) in the synced settings, so every device shows the same Pinned section. Today's chat pins move over as chat-level pins. Paseo's own workspace pin stays separate: it orders Paseo's default view, and this view does not need it.
